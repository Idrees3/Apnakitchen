Food & Allergen Disclaimer

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Halal representation

Apna Kitchen prepares food intended for Muslim customers seeking halal options in Frisco, Texas. For questions about meat sources or preparation for a specific dish, ask on WhatsApp before you order so we can answer accurately.

Home-style / small kitchen

Food is prepared in a small kitchen environment, not a full-service restaurant dining room. We follow cleanliness practices appropriate to our operation. Customers should follow any local cottage-food or home-food regulations that apply to their situation; we will share applicable pickup instructions on WhatsApp.

Allergens

Our recipes may include or come into contact with common allergens, including but not limited to:

  • Wheat / gluten (for example when served with bread)
  • Dairy / yogurt / ghee / butter
  • Tree nuts or nut pastes (including in some qorma-style gravies)
  • Soy, mustard, sesame, or spices that may trigger sensitivities

Because we cook multiple dishes in one kitchen, cross-contact can occur. If you have a severe allergy, message us before ordering — we may decline an order we cannot prepare safely.

Food safety after pickup

  • Refrigerate perishable leftovers promptly.
  • Reheat thoroughly before eating.
  • Frozen kababs should stay frozen until cooked; cook fully before serving.
  • Do not consume food that smells or looks unusual.

Nutrition

Menu descriptions are culinary, not medical or nutrition advice. Portion weights (for example 1 kg / 2 lb) are approximate tray sizes as listed on our menu.
