How to order Apna Kitchen

We keep ordering simple for busy Muslim families in Frisco: WhatsApp message today, fresh food ready for pickup tomorrow.

Browse the menu

Choose trays from mutton, chicken & rice, or frozen kababs. Note the unit (kg/lb, whole chicken, or dozen).

WhatsApp your order

Message +1 (469) 891-1406 with dish names, quantities, preferred pickup day/time, and your name.

Confirm & pick up

We confirm availability and pickup details. Food is cooked fresh — please arrive at the agreed time near Sherbrooke Drive, Frisco.

Important rules

  • Orders must be placed one day in advance.
  • Pickup only for now — delivery is not offered yet.
  • WhatsApp only for orders and inquiries (no online cart).
  • Payment method is confirmed on WhatsApp before pickup.
  • Large catering or event trays: message early so we can plan.
